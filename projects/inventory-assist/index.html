Overview

Two roles, two entirely different products

Role is not a permission checkbox bolted onto one dashboard — it decides the navigation, the routes and the whole surface a user ever sees.

Administrator

/admin/*

Owns configuration. Everything that defines what the business stocks, where it lives, and who supplies it.

  • KPI dashboard with low-stock and overstock alerting
  • Products & variants — per-variant pricing overrides
  • 3-step CSV/XLSX bulk import with row-level validation
  • Categories, suppliers and lead-time tracking
  • Multi-warehouse stock with bin locations and capacity
  • Purchase orders through a five-state lifecycle
  • Automatic reorder suggestions → convert to PO
  • Excel exports, and user management with activate, deactivate and guarded delete

Stock clerk

/clerk/*

Owns operations. A deliberately narrow surface built for speed on the warehouse floor, with no configuration to get lost in.

  • Inventory browser across every warehouse
  • Status at a glance — OK, Low Stock, Overstock
  • Cart-style multi-item transaction entry
  • Sales, receipts, adjustments, returns, cancellations
  • Live stock preview before a movement is committed
  • Receipt generation on sales and returns
  • Searchable, filterable transaction history
  • Low-stock notification bell

Features

What it actually does

Products & variants

Full CRUD over a catalogue where every product carries variants by size and colour, each able to override price and cost independently.

Bulk import wizard

Three-step CSV/XLSX import: upload, validate, commit. Headers are normalized, every row is checked, and errors report per row and per field.

Multi-warehouse stock

Three locations with independent bin assignments, capacity ceilings and per-variant stock records — the same SKU can be low in one site and overstocked in another.

Purchase orders

Supplier-scoped orders with line items and subtotals, moving through Pending → Approved → Shipped → Delivered, with cancellation.

Reorder engine

Suggestions raised automatically when stock drops under the reorder point, each tied to a preferred supplier and convertible into a PO in one action.

Transactions & receipts

Cart-style entry across five movement types, with a live stock preview, receipt generation, and a searchable history.

Reports & export

Inventory and transaction data exported to Excel straight from the browser, including one-click exports off the dashboard KPI cards.

Role-based access

Route guards resolve the user's role and redirect to the right dashboard, so a clerk cannot reach configuration screens by typing a URL.

Under the hood

Decisions worth pointing at

The parts that took thought rather than typing.

Validation happens before anything is written

The import parser normalizes headers (Unit Price → unit_price), enforces eleven required columns, rejects negative or fractional quantities, and catches duplicate variant SKUs inside the same file. Each row is tagged new, update, new_category or error, so the operator reviews the whole outcome before committing a single change.

Stock math is directional

Movement type decides the sign: sales subtract, receipts and returns add, adjustments carry their own signed quantity. The projected result is shown live while the clerk types, and a sale larger than the quantity on hand is rejected at entry rather than at submit.

Server state, not global state

Data flows through TanStack React Query rather than a hand-rolled store. Today it resolves from an in-memory mock module; swapping in a real API means changing the query functions and nothing else in the component tree.

Status is derived, never stored

OK, Low Stock and Overstock are computed from qtyOnHand against the variant's reorderPoint and maxStockLevel. There is no status column to fall out of sync with the number it describes.
